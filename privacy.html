Namazing – Privacy policy

Effective: 23 November 2026 · Developer: Ahmetcan Yılmaz · Contact: acy.apps@gmail.com

Namazing needs no account and contains no ads or analytics; your records stay on your phone.

Stored on your phone

Your prayer log, qada (missed prayer) balance and history, chosen city, mosque log and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us and we run no server that holds them.

If your phone's own backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), this data is included in that backup. Those backups live in your Google or Apple account; we have no access to them.

Prayer times are calculated offline on your phone. Location permission is used only for "Find my location" and the mosque search; your location is not stored or tracked.

Backup file

When you export a backup in Settings, the file is created on your phone and you choose where to send it (your own email, your cloud folder). The app never sends it anywhere by itself.

Notifications

Prayer-time notifications are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent for them.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and asks no one for personal information.

Your rights

All your data is on your phone: deleting the app deletes it. To have a feedback message you sent us deleted, write to acy.apps@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published at this address with a new effective date.
